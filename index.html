Some works that have inspired me:

Game: Rimworld

A 2D sandbox colony-building and survival game. The amount of emergent gameplay, player expression, and replayability still blows me away. A huge inspiration for why I got into game design.

A screenshot from my current run.

Game: Diceomancer

One of the best roguelike deckbuilders that I've played. The artstyle and gameplay have sered as an example of card game design.

Novel: The Fisherman by John Langan

A very well-written horror novel about loss. I looked to this book as an example when I was writing my text-based game, hoping to achieve a similar quality.
